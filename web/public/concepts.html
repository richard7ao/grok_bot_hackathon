HotDrop motion concepts

Six small interaction ideas for review, each rebuilt in HotDrop's own look. Every demo is live: click, hover or scroll. Techniques were studied on usedarwin.app; nothing here reuses its copy or assets.

1. Marker sweep headline

Inspired by
Darwin home and onboarding: a flat highlight block sits behind half the headline.
In HotDrop
Landing hero: a cobalt-soft marker wipes in once under "before it's gone."

Wear it before it's gone.

2. Live takes feed

Inspired by
Darwin home hero: a simulated live list where one row lifts into the spotlight in turn.
In HotDrop
Landing hero, next to the phone: recent takes and scores rotate, so the page feels busy before anyone taps.

    3. Pulse ring on live pins

    Inspired by
    Darwin's live-status dot: a box-shadow ring expands and fades every 1.8 s.
    In HotDrop
    /mobile map: live drops pulse, sold-out drops go grey and still. Landing "Live" badges use the same ring. Click a pin to sell one.

    4. Your stylist is looking

    Inspired by
    Darwin's mascot: a slow bob, an occasional blink, and three typing dots while it works.
    In HotDrop
    /mobile queue screen while the take waits for a score. Click to reveal a score.

    Your stylist is watching your take

    5. Product breaks the word

    Inspired by
    Darwin's demo store: a giant display word with the product sitting through the letters.
    In HotDrop
    Landing "Live now" header and the drop sheet hero. Move the pointer: the photo tilts toward it.

    DROP

    6. Tilt that settles on scroll

    Inspired by
    Darwin home: the product dashboard is framed as a slab seen slightly from below.
    In HotDrop
    Landing: the Studio preview starts tilted back and lies flat as it scrolls into view.
