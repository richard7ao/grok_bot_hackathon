How a drop works

  1. 01

    Walk to the pin

    Get within 150 m of the pin on the map and the drop unlocks.

  2. 02

    Film 10–20 s

    Show your outfit and how you'd style the piece, on camera.

  3. 03

    AI review

    An AI stylist checks your take against a short rubric — three tries per drop.

  4. 04

    Hold, post, buy

    Pass and hold the item for 10 minutes — post the Reel, then buy.
